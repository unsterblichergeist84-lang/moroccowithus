# Keyword research - Morocco With Us

## How this was done, and its limits

Research was done by searching the live web for the phrases travellers use and
reading what the results page looks like for each (what operators title their
pages, how long trips are, what gets bundled). **This gives us phrasing and
intent. It does not give search volume or keyword difficulty** - no keyword tool
(Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush) was available. Before investing in
more content, validate the "priority" terms below in one of those and in Google
Search Console once the site is live.

Searches run (October 2026): private 3-day Fes to Merzouga; 3-day Marrakech to
Merzouga; Casablanca / Tangier / Chefchaouen private itineraries; 10-day Morocco
from Casablanca; Tangier to Chefchaouen day trips; Marrakech to Essaouira;
Fes to Chefchaouen transfer; Fes-Meknes-Volubilis day trip; Hassan II Mosque
visits; Akchour waterfalls; Todra Gorge and Dades Valley from Marrakech.

## What the results pages told us

1. **The pattern is `[N]-day [private] [tour] from [city] to [place]`.**
   Titles almost always lead with the duration and the departure city, and use
   "private" heavily ("Private 3 Day Desert Tour From Marrakech To Merzouga
   Dunes", "Fes to Sahara Desert Tour 3 Days: Private 3-day Morocco Tour").
2. **Departure city + destination is the unit of search**, which is why the site
   is organised by departure city. Each tour page should own one such phrase.
3. **Results are dominated by marketplaces** (Viator, GetYourGuide, TripAdvisor)
   plus many small local operators. Head terms like "Morocco tours" are not
   realistic; the opening is specific long-tail phrases and private-tour intent.
4. **Standard itineraries are crowded.** "3 days Fes to Merzouga" and
   "3 days Marrakech to Merzouga" have dozens of near-identical competitors.
   Our differentiators - private only, slower pacing, two nights in the dunes -
   belong in titles and descriptions.
5. **Informational long-tail is open.** Practical questions (Hassan II Mosque
   tickets and times, Akchour trail options, Todra vs Dades, Essaouira as a day
   trip vs overnight) are answered mostly by thin blogs and listings.
6. **Price words are everywhere** ("from EUR 150", "prices start from"). We do not
   publish prices by design, so we deliberately do not target price-modified
   keywords. Be aware this forgoes some traffic.

## Where the titles live

`src/content/seo.ts` holds a search title (60 characters or fewer, no brand
suffix) for all 24 tours and 30 posts, plus meta descriptions for the tours.
Page headings (H1) are unchanged. Edit that file to adjust titles; the pages
pick it up automatically.

## Suggested next steps

1. Validate the priority keywords for volume and difficulty (Keyword Planner is
   free; Ahrefs or Semrush if you have them) and swap any that are too weak or too
   competitive.
2. Once live, connect Google Search Console and re-check after 4-8 weeks to see
   which phrases actually trigger impressions.
3. Add FAQs to the high-value tour pages using the questions above (the site
   already emits FAQ structured data).
4. Gaps worth new posts: "Fes to Merzouga: what the drive is like", "Marrakech
   to Merzouga in 3 days vs 5", "Best time to visit Chefchaouen", "Essaouira day
   trip or overnight".
