Tangier sits where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, nine miles from Spain.
It has long been a crossroads, and that shows in its architecture, food and
mix of languages.

## The kasbah and medina

The kasbah sits on the hill above the old town, with views across the strait. The
medina below is compact and easy to explore, with its main market square, small
cafes and narrow lanes.

## Cap Spartel and the Caves of Hercules

A short drive west brings you to Cap Spartel, where the two seas meet, and the
nearby Caves of Hercules, a sea cave with an opening shaped, supposedly, like
the outline of Africa. Sunset from here is the classic outing.

## A good starting point

Tangier has an airport, a port and a high-speed rail link south, which makes it
an easy place to begin or end a trip. From here you can go on to the blue lanes
of Chefchaouen, Fes or all the way to the Sahara.
