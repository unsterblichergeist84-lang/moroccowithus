Fes el Bali is one of the largest car-free urban areas in the world, and it is
easy to feel lost in it. The good news is that getting a little lost is part of
the point - as long as you plan around a few fixed anchors.

## Allow two days, not one

A single day gets you the headline sights. Two lets you see them at a sensible
pace and leave a block of time for wandering. The medina is
[listed by UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/170/) and the old city is
dense - the distances are short, but the lanes slow everything down.

## Anchors worth planning around

- **The medersas.** Bou Inania and Attarine are the two most rewarding - quiet,
  carved and easy to visit in a morning.
- **The Qaraouiyine quarter.** The area around the old university and mosque is
  the oldest part of the city.
- **The tanneries.** Go in the morning, when the vats are in use and the light
  is better.
- **A rooftop.** Several riads and cafes look out over the medina - useful for
  getting your bearings.

## Go with a guide the first time

Not forever - just once. A good local guide shortens the learning curve and

## When to go

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable. Summer afternoons are hot inside the
walls - see [when to visit Morocco](/blog/when-to-visit-morocco).
