"Private" is a word a lot of operators use. It is worth being precise about what
is on offer.

## What it means with us

- **No other travellers.** You are not joined by strangers at any point. The
  vehicle, the guide and the driver are yours for the trip.
- **No fixed departure dates.** The trip starts when you want it to. There is no
  schedule to fit around.
- **The itinerary is yours to shape.** The routes on this site are starting
  points. You move the days around, add nights, drop a stop, slow the pace.
- **One point of contact.** The person who plans the trip is reachable while you
  travel.

## What it does not mean

- It does not mean luxury by default. Private is about control, not thread
  count. We match the accommodation to what you want and what you are spending.
- It does not mean a guide with you every hour. In the cities you have a guide
  for set half-days or days; the rest of the time is yours. The driver is always
  there.
- It does not mean no planning on your part. The more you tell us about how you
  like to travel, the better the draft.

## Why it costs what it costs

A private trip is priced from its own itinerary - the actual nights, the actual
driving, the guides you actually use. That is why there are no package prices on
this site: there is no package. See
[how many days you need](/blog/how-many-days-in-morocco) and
[driving distances](/blog/driving-distances-in-morocco) for the two things that
move the number most.
