The north gets skipped by travellers heading straight for the desert, which is a
shame. It is a different Morocco - Andalusian, green, cooler - and four days is
enough to feel it.

## The shape of it

1. **Two nights in Tangier.** The medina, the kasbah, Cap Spartel at sunset.
2. **The mountain road.** Two to three hours up into the Rif.
3. **Two nights in Chefchaouen.** The blue lanes early, then the Akchour
   waterfalls in the
   [Talassemtane National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talassemtane_National_Park)
   as a half-day walk.

## What to bring

Walking shoes with grip for Akchour, a layer for the evenings, and cash - the
mountain villages are not card country. Our [packing guide](/blog/packing-for-morocco)
goes into more detail.
