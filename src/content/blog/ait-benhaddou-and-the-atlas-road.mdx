The leg from Marrakech across the High Atlas to Ait Benhaddou is often treated
as a transfer. It deserves to be treated as a day.

## The Tizi n'Tichka pass

The [Tizi n'Tichka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tizi_n%27Tichka) climbs to
2,260 metres through switchbacks and Berber villages. The old route is slower and
more scenic; a newer stretch has smoothed some of the worst bends. Either way it
is about three and a half hours to Ait Benhaddou with stops - see the
[full distance table](/blog/driving-distances-in-morocco).

## Ait Benhaddou

A ksar - a fortified village of packed earth - stacked against a hillside above
the Ounila river, and a [UNESCO World Heritage site](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/444).
Parts of it are nearly a thousand years old; a few families still live inside. It has stood in for ancient Jerusalem, Egypt and half a dozen
fictional cities on film, but it holds up entirely on its own.

Go late in the afternoon when the day groups have left and the light is on the
west face, or early before they arrive. Cross the river and climb to the granary
at the top for the view back over the valley.

## Onward

From here the road runs east through Ouarzazate - the Taourirt kasbah, the film
studios - and the Skoura palm oasis, then along the valley of a thousand kasbahs
towards the gorges and the desert.

Both the [Marrakech loop](/tours/marrakech-sahara-loop) and trips built from
scratch use this route. A night at Ait Benhaddou rather than a quick stop makes
the whole southern stretch better.
