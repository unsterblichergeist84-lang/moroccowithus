Roads in Morocco are mostly good, but the distances are long and the mountain
sections are slow. These are realistic times with a private driver, including
normal stops but not long ones.

## Common legs

| From | To | Driving time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marrakech | Ait Benhaddou | 3.5 hours |
| Ait Benhaddou | Ouarzazate | 45 minutes |
| Ouarzazate | Tinghir (Todgha Gorge) | 3 hours |
| Tinghir | Merzouga | 3 hours |
| Fes | Midelt | 3.5 hours |
| Midelt | Merzouga | 3.5 hours |
| Marrakech | Essaouira | 3 hours |
| Fes | Chefchaouen | 4 hours |
| Chefchaouen | Tangier | 2 hours |
| Marrakech | Fes (direct) | 7 hours |

## What this means for planning

- **Marrakech to the desert and back** is two long driving days each way. Doing
  it as a loop in under five days is a lot of car.
- **Point to point beats a loop.** Flying into Fes and out of Marrakech (or vice
  versa) turns a there-and-back desert trip into a one-way crossing and saves a
  full day.
- **The [Tizi n'Tichka pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tizi_n%27Tichka)**
  between Marrakech and Ouarzazate is spectacular but slow, and can close
  briefly after winter snow.

## A note on "quick stops"

Every viewpoint, argan cooperative and kasbah adds fifteen to thirty minutes. A
day with "a few stops" on a 4-hour leg is really a 6-hour day. We build the
itinerary around that rather than pretending otherwise.
