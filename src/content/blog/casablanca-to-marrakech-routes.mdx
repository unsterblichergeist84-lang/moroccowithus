Most travellers take the motorway from Casablanca to Marrakech, which takes
about three hours. There is a slower and more interesting alternative.

## The motorway

Straightforward and fast, with services along the route. A private car or
driver makes this a comfortable half-day.

## The coast route via Essaouira

Heading south down the Atlantic coast and then inland adds a night or two, but
you gain a stretch of coast, the fortified port of Essaouira and some argan
country on the way. It suits travellers who have more than a few days.

## Which to choose

- **Short on time:** take the motorway and give the days to Marrakech.
- **Seven days or more:** add Essaouira. Our
  [Casablanca to Marrakech via Essaouira tour](/tours/casablanca-to-marrakech-via-essaouira)
  covers this in five days.
