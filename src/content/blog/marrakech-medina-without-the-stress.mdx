Marrakech has a reputation for being intense. Most of that comes from visiting
at the wrong time of day, with no plan.

## Go early

The medina is quiet and cooler in the morning. The Bahia Palace and the Saadian
Tombs are far more pleasant before the tour groups arrive.

## Break up the souks

An hour or two in the souks is enough at a time. Retreat to a courtyard cafe or
your riad for a break, then return in the late afternoon.

## Jemaa el-Fnaa at dusk

The main square comes alive in the evening, with food stalls, musicians and
storytellers. Go for a short visit at sunset, ideally from a rooftop terrace, and
decide from there how long you want to stay.
