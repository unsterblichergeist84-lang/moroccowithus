The Tizi n'Tichka is the main road over the High Atlas between Marrakech and the
south. It reaches roughly 2,200 metres and is usually open all year, though snow
can close it briefly in winter.

## The drive

Leaving Marrakech, the road crosses the plain and then climbs through winding
valleys. Allow at least two hours to the pass itself, longer with stops. Views
open up on the descent, as the landscape turns from green to dry and red.

## Where to stop

- **A Berber village.** A short walk and tea in a village on the northern side
  gives context to the landscape.
- **Viewpoints near the summit.** Good for photographs but expect roadside stalls.
- **[Ait Benhaddou](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/444/).** A fortified village of
  earthen buildings, reached a couple of hours beyond the pass.

## Plan for the road, not the clock

The route is slow in places and trucks are common, so leave early and treat the
drive as part of the day. See also
[driving distances in Morocco](/blog/driving-distances-in-morocco).
