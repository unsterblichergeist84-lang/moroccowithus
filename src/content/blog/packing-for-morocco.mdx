Most trips here move through several climates in a week. You do not need a lot,
but you need the right things.

## Clothing

- **Layers over bulk.** Medina days can be warm and the same night in the desert
  or the mountains can be near freezing. A light insulated jacket packs small and
  earns its place.
- **Covered but light.** Loose trousers and tops that cover the shoulders are
  comfortable in the heat and appropriate in rural areas and religious sites. No
  need for anything strict in the cities.
- **One smart-ish outfit** if you like eating somewhere nice in Marrakech.

## Feet

Shoes with grip. Medina lanes are uneven and often slick, and any walk near
water - the Akchour falls, the
[Todgha gorge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todgha_Gorge) - needs a proper
sole. Sandals for the camp.

## The desert kit

A head torch, sunglasses, a scarf you can wrap over your face in wind, and lip
balm. Sunscreen year round.

## Documents and money

Cash matters outside the cities - the mountain villages and many small
restaurants do not take cards. Bring a card for the cities and keep some dirham
for everywhere else.

## What to leave

A drone (permits are difficult), heavy camera gear you will not use, and more
than one book - the riads usually have a shelf.
