The Todgha and Dades valleys are among the most scenic stretches of southern
Morocco, and they sit naturally on the road between the Atlas and the dunes of
Merzouga.

## The Todgha Gorge

Near the town of Tinghir, the river has cut a narrow canyon with rock walls
rising well over a hundred metres. You can walk along the valley floor, which is
cool and shaded in the morning. The palm groves of Tinghir itself are worth a
slow walk.

## The Dades valley

Further west the Dades valley is wider and carved with unusual rock
formations. The road follows the river past a string of kasbahs - the so-called
road of a thousand kasbahs.

## How to include them

They work well as an overnight stop between Ait Benhaddou and Merzouga, or as
the destination of a shorter trip from Marrakech. Our
[Southern Oases & Gorges tour](/tours/marrakech-southern-oases-and-gorges) is a
four-day version.

## Practical notes

Mornings are best for walking. The roads are good but slow, so plan stops rather
than long distances. See [Ait Benhaddou and the Atlas road](/blog/ait-benhaddou-and-the-atlas-road)
for the first part of the route.
