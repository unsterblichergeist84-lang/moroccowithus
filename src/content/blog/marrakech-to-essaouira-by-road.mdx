Essaouira is about three hours from Marrakech and the road is straightforward,
which makes it one of the easiest coastal escapes in Morocco.

## The drive

The road runs west across the plain and then into argan country, where goats
famously climb the trees. You will pass roadside cooperatives selling argan oil
and cosmetics - a stop at a women's cooperative is worth making if you want to
see how the oil is produced.

## Essaouira itself

The old town is a UNESCO-listed
[fortified port](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/753/), laid out on a grid
behind sea walls. The Skala ramparts, the fishing harbour and the medina are all
compact and walkable. The wind keeps temperatures lower than inland and gives
the town its slower pace.

## How long to stay

Two nights is ideal: one full day is enough for the ramparts, harbour and a long
lunch, with time left for the beach. Our
