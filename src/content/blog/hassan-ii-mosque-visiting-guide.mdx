The Hassan II Mosque stands on the Atlantic shoreline in Casablanca and was
completed in 1993. Its minaret is among the tallest in the world, and part of
the building extends over the sea.

## Visiting

Non-Muslim visitors can enter on guided tours, which run on a schedule through
the day and usually close on Friday mornings and during prayer times. Check the
current timetable before you go, as it can change.

## What you see

The tour takes in the vast prayer hall, which can hold tens of thousands of
worshippers, the carved cedar and plasterwork, the marble and zellige tile
work, and the hammam beneath. Craftspeople from across the country worked on
it.

## Practical tips

- Dress modestly - shoulders and knees covered.
- Allow about an hour for the guided tour.
- Book ahead in busy periods, or ask your guide to arrange it.
- Visit in the morning for the best light on the exterior.
