Meknes and Volubilis are close to each other and about an hour west of Fes,
which makes them one of the most efficient day trips in the country.

## Volubilis

[Volubilis](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/836/) is the best-preserved Roman site
in Morocco, standing in open farmland. Mosaics remain in place in several of the
houses, and the triumphal arch and basilica give a sense of scale. Go early or
late for better light and fewer visitors.

## Meknes

Meknes was an imperial capital under Moulay Ismail in the seventeenth century.
It is calmer than Fes and more spread out. The gate of Bab Mansour is the
highlight; the granaries and stables hint at the size of the original court.

## Fitting both in

Do Volubilis first, while it is cooler, then Meknes for lunch and a short walk.
