## When to go

The dunes people picture are [Erg Chebbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erg_Chebbi),
the sand sea at Merzouga. It has two comfortable windows: **March to May** and
**October to November**. Summer days on the dunes are punishing; deep winter
nights are cold but the light is extraordinary and the sites are empty. There is
more on this in [when to visit Morocco](/blog/when-to-visit-morocco).

## How much driving

Morocco is larger than it looks on a map. A Marrakech-to-Merzouga loop is two
long driving days each way. Going point to point - flying into one city and out
of another - buys you a day or two back. See
[driving distances in Morocco](/blog/driving-distances-in-morocco) for the real
numbers.

### A rough sense of distances

| Leg | Driving time |
| --- | --- |
| Marrakech to Ait Benhaddou | 3.5 hours |
| Ait Benhaddou to Tinghir | 3.5 hours |
| Tinghir to Merzouga | 3 hours |

## Pace

Most people try to see too much. Two nights in the desert instead of one changes
the trip completely - there is time for the light to do its work. Our
[Fes to the Sahara](/tours/fes-sahara-crossing) and
[Marrakech loop](/tours/marrakech-sahara-loop) itineraries both build in the
second night, and [a night in the Sahara](/blog/a-night-in-the-sahara) covers
what the camp is actually like.
