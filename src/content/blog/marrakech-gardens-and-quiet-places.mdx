Marrakech is as much about its quiet places as its loud ones.

## Gardens

- **Jardin Majorelle.** The cobalt-blue garden created by Jacques Majorelle and
  later restored. Go when it opens to avoid queues.
- **Le Jardin Secret.** A restored riad garden in the medina with a calm,
  geometric layout.
- **The Menara and Agdal gardens.** Larger, older and much less visited, with
  olive groves and a pavilion by a basin.

## Courtyards and riads

Many riads have inner courtyards that are quiet at any hour. A long breakfast or
mint tea there is a good counterweight to the souks.

## Hammams

A traditional hammam is a half-day pause: steam, a scrub and a rest. Choose a
reputable one and ask your guide or riad for advice.
