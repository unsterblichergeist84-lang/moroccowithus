Rabat is the political capital, an hour north of Casablanca. It is orderly,
walkable and much less hectic than the other big cities.

## Kasbah of the Udayas

A twelfth-century fortress above the river mouth, with whitewashed lanes and
blue doors. The Andalusian gardens inside are a quiet place for a walk, and the
terrace overlooks the Atlantic.

## The Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum

The unfinished minaret of an enormous twelfth-century mosque stands next to the
Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Together they make a good short visit, with the field
of columns in front of the tower adding to the sense of scale.

## The medina and the Chellah

Rabat's medina is small and relaxed. The Chellah, a Roman and medieval site on
the edge of the city, is worth a visit if you have time.
