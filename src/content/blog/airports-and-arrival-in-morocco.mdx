Morocco has several international airports, and choosing the right one can save
a day of driving. Our tours start from four cities.

## Casablanca

The main hub, with the most flights. From the airport you can take a train
toward Rabat and Casablanca city, or transfer by private driver. It suits
itineraries that head north or east, or that begin in the imperial cities. See
[our Casablanca tours](/tours/casablanca-and-rabat).

## Marrakech

A popular gateway for visitors heading to the Atlas, the southern oases or the
desert. The city is close to the airport, so you can be in your riad shortly
after landing.

## Fes

A smaller airport with a growing list of European routes. A good start for the
imperial cities and the Sahara crossing.

## Tangier

Useful for the north and for travellers coming from Spain by ferry. It has a
high-speed train connection to Rabat and Casablanca.

## One-way trips

Flying into one city and out of another reduces the driving - for example
into Tangier and out of Marrakech. See [how many days in Morocco](/blog/how-many-days-in-morocco)
for planning, and contact us if you want help choosing the route.
