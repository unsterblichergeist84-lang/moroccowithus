The country is larger than it looks on a map, and the interesting parts are
spread out. Here is what actually fits.

## A week (7 days)

One city and the desert, done properly. For example: three nights in Marrakech
or Fes, two driving days to
[Erg Chebbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erg_Chebbi), two nights in the dunes.
Or two cities and no desert. Trying to do both cities and the Sahara in a week
means long days in the car - see
[driving distances](/blog/driving-distances-in-morocco) - and not much time
anywhere.

## Ten days

The comfortable minimum for cities plus desert. Fes, a slow crossing of the
Middle Atlas, two nights at Erg Chebbi, then Marrakech - with a night in the
southern valleys rather than one enormous drive.

## Two weeks

Now you can add the north or the coast without rushing. Two weeks lets you do
[Tangier and Chefchaouen](/blog/north-morocco-in-four-days), the imperial cities,
the desert and Marrakech, with a pause in Essaouira at the end. The
[destinations page](/destinations) shows how the regions connect.

## The rule we keep coming back to

Add a night before you add a place. A second night in the desert, or an
unplanned afternoon in a medina, is almost always worth more than another town
on the list.
