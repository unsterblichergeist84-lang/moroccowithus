Akchour is a village in the Talassemtane national park, about forty minutes by
road from Chefchaouen, and the starting point for one of the best easy hikes in
northern Morocco.

## The walk

A path follows the river upstream through oak and pine forest, passing
swimming pools and small cafes. The main walk reaches the Grand Cascade, a
waterfall in a green canyon. A longer route continues to the Bridge of God, a
natural rock arch over the gorge.

## How long

Allow half a day for the waterfall and a full day if you continue to the bridge.
The terrain is uneven in places, so wear proper shoes.

## When to go

Spring and early summer have the most water. In August the river can be low, but
the pools are popular. Start early in warm weather.
