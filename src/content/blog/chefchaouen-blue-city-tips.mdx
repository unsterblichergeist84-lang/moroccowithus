Chefchaouen is the blue town in the Rif mountains, and it is every bit as
photogenic as the pictures suggest. It is also busy at midday, so a little
planning helps.

## Go early or late

Day-trippers arrive from late morning. Walk the lanes at first light or in the
evening, when the town is quiet and the light is soft.

## Two nights, not one

An overnight stay gives you mornings and evenings in the medina without the
crowds. A second night lets you add a hike or a day at Akchour - see
[our Akchour guide](/blog/akchour-waterfalls-hike).

## What to see

- The plaza and the old kasbah, with its small museum.
- The Spanish Mosque, a short walk up the hill, for sunset views.
- The lanes of the old medina, where blue gives way to white and ochre.

## Respect the residents

People live here. Ask before photographing them or their doorways, and keep
your voice down in residential lanes.

## Getting there

Chefchaouen is about two hours from Tangier and four to five from Fes.
