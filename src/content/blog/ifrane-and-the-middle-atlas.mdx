Between Fes and the desert the road climbs into the Middle Atlas, and the
country changes. There are cedar forests, small lakes and a town that looks like
it belongs in the Alps.

## Ifrane

Ifrane was built in the 1930s as a hill station, with steep roofs and tidy
parks. It is often called the "Switzerland of Morocco" and in winter it can be
snowy. It is a pleasant stop for a coffee and a short walk.

## The cedar forest near Azrou

The forest around Azrou holds some of the oldest cedars in the country, along
with troops of [Barbary macaques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbary_macaque).
Keep your distance and do not feed them - it is bad for the animals and they can
be assertive.
