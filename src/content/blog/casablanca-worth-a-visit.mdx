Casablanca is Morocco's largest city and its main airport hub, and many
travellers pass straight through. It is not a postcard city, but it rewards a
short stay.

## What it is

A working port and business capital, with long boulevards, an Atlantic corniche
and one of the most important modern buildings in the country - the Hassan II
Mosque.

## What to see

- **The Hassan II Mosque.** Guided tours let non-Muslim visitors inside - see our
  [Hassan II Mosque guide](/blog/hassan-ii-mosque-visiting-guide).
- **Art deco architecture.** The city centre has a notable collection of
  early twentieth-century buildings, many of them being restored.
- **The Corniche.** A promenade along the sea, best at sunset.
- **The old medina.** Small, and less polished than elsewhere, but real.

## How much time to give it

One full day is enough for the main sights. If you arrive on a late flight,
spend the first night here and add a day. Our
[Casablanca & Rabat tour](/tours/casablanca-and-rabat) is built around this.

## Where to go next

From Casablanca, Rabat is an hour north and Marrakech about three hours south.
